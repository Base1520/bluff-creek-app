Church calendar

Keep everyone on the same page.

The church calendar is maintained in iCloud. Open Apple Calendar or iCloud Calendar with an account invited by the calendar owner to add or change church events.

Open iCloud Calendar ↗

How editor access works

The calendar owner invites each editor through Apple Calendar sharing and enables editing. Creek Office access and iCloud permissions are separate. Use Apple Calendar on iPhone, or iCloud Calendar on a tablet or computer.

Review community event requests with the calendar owner, then make approved changes in iCloud so everyone sees the same schedule.
