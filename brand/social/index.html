Captions, cadence, and the rules that matter

Caption formulas — fill in the blanks

  • Sunday invite: "Doors open at 9. [One warm sentence about what happens.] Come as you are — we'd love to meet you. 1706 Highway 63, Clinton."
  • Sermon quote: "[The quote.] — Pastor Cole, [passage], this Sunday at the Creek. Full message at the link in bio."
  • Scripture: Post the verse and the reference. Say nothing else. Let it sit.
  • Event: "[What it is.] [Day, date, time.] [Where.] [Who it's for.] [What to bring, or 'just bring yourself.']"
  • Youth: "6 on the 63. Wednesday, 6:00, fellowship building. [What's happening.] Bring a friend."
  • Recap / photo dump: "[What we did.] Grateful for [who]. [One line of why it mattered.]"

Rhythm — three posts a week beats seven

  • Thursday: what's coming Sunday.
  • Sunday morning: the invite, posted early.
  • Monday or Tuesday: a line from the sermon, or photos from the weekend.
  • Stories any time — they're low stakes and don't need to be perfect.

Always

  • Ivory backgrounds most of the time. Pine and gold are accents, not the whole post.
  • Kidz @ the Creek is spelled with a z. That's the church's own spelling.
  • Ministry names always read [Ministry] @ the Creek — never "at the Creek", never "BCBC Youth".
  • One idea per post. If it needs two headlines, it's two posts.
  • Real photos of real people beat stock every single time.

Never

  • Don't recolor, stretch, outline, or add effects to the logo.
  • Don't give a ministry its own logo, icon, or mascot — accent color only.
  • Don't use the green from the little LA 63 highway sign (#006B54) anywhere else. It belongs to that marker only.
  • Don't post photos of children without a parent's okay.
  • Don't post photos from international mission work. Ever — it can expose locations and the people who host us.
  • Don't use canned Christian stock art or free-clipart crosses.
