Homestead

A rural country church, true to its roots and current to this generation. Light ground, deep pine, one gold accent. Everything below is the single source of truth — the church app, the website, print, and slides all pull from this page.

Proportion is the whole trick. If a layout feels heavy, there is too much pine. If it feels loud, too much gold. Supporting neutrals: soft text #5E574C, faint #938A79, borders #E4DAC8, wheat tint #F6EED9, pine deep #2E4636.

Sign green is not a brand color. #006B54 is the real highway sign's green — a found object. It appears only inside the official LA 63 marker, and the marker is never tinted.

Naming

“@ the Creek”

The master word is the Creek. “Bluff Creek Baptist Church” stays on the sign and on formal documents. Every ministry uses one construction, and ministries differ by accent color only — same type, same creek, never a new logo, icon, or mascot.

The creek & the wordmark

Always

  • Use the actual brushstroke lifted from the logo — never a redrawn wavy line
  • Gold #B08636 by default (~90% of uses); pine on white; ivory reversed on pine
  • Points right. Minimum width 48px. Once per screen at full strength
  • Wordmark: black on ivory or white; ivory reversed on pine only
  • Clear space around the wordmark equals the cap-height of the B

Never

  • Recolor, shadow, outline, stretch, rotate, or split the wordmark
  • Use the logo script as a typeface anywhere else
  • Write “at the Creek”, “Creek Kids”, or “BCBC Youth”
  • Use “@” outside the lockup
  • Give a ministry its own logo, icon, or mascot
The 63 — a quiet thread

The church sits on Louisiana Highway 63, and the number runs underneath the brand rather than on top of it. The rules: it never competes with the creek, never becomes a logo or an app icon, and gets one wink per surface.

DeviceHow it is used
Route markerThe official Louisiana LA 63 shield (public domain), always #006B54, never recolored, tinted, outlined, or redrawn. Small by the address, in the app header, on the pew card, and in the site footer. On social graphics it is the hero device — set at roughly 18% of the card width, bottom-right, in place of the numeral watermark.
Numeral watermarkA huge Bitter “63” at 5–9% opacity, cropped off the corner of a pine block. Never behind body text.
