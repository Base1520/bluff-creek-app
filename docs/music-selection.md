# Songs @ the Creek — selection and upkeep

## Direction confirmed September 7, 2026

The pastor asked for Shane & Shane, Phil Wickham, the Gettys and modern hymns with substantial, biblically grounded lyrics. After discussing overlapping credits, he explicitly excluded **Bethel, Hillsong and Elevation** and allowed Brandon Lake to be omitted. Leave Brandon Lake off the starter playlist. These are Bluff Creek’s curation choices, not a claim that an artist’s entire catalogue has been assessed.

Screen the specific composition **and** recording: lyrics, credited writers, publishing entities, featured artists and any medley additions. A cover by a preferred artist does not remove a composition’s credits. Do not add songs carrying the excluded groups’ writing/publishing or recording ties. Shared use of a general distributor or rights administrator alone does not establish a church affiliation. Hold unclear credits for review rather than marking them cleared.

For example, [Gratitude](https://bethelmusic.com/resources/house-of-miracles/gratitude) and [Hymn of Heaven](https://bethelmusic.com/resources/homecoming-live/hymn-of-heaven) have Bethel publishing credits; [Praise featuring Brandon Lake](https://www.elevationworship.com/music/praise) is an Elevation release. These are excluded from this list, including covers of those compositions. This does not make a judgment about every lyric or person involved.

## Choose songs that serve this church

Use Bluff Creek’s stated Baptist Faith & Message 2000 foundation when reading lyrics. Favor clear praise of the triune God, Christ’s person and saving work, the cross and bodily resurrection, grace, repentance, faith and faithful discipleship. Include lament and honest prayer alongside assurance and joy. A song need not state every doctrine; its central claims should agree with Scripture without requiring a strained explanation.

The first list follows a listening arc: praise → Christ’s saving work → trust through difficulty → hope and sending. Psalms and songs of patient trust support the Habakkuk series without implying they were written about Habakkuk or are the actual Sunday set. Artist popularity and another church’s playlist are discovery aids, not approval evidence.
