# Membership history, guests and deacon care

Creek Office now has a working interface and a prepared private database migration for long-term membership history and care follow-up. It is still a feature-branch proposal: no live staff backend, Google Sheets write-back, OCR service, or external reminder delivery has been activated.

## Source of truth and the existing Google ledger

The existing Google records remain the working source while their scope and import mapping are reconciled. On September 7, 2026, the original roll's visible `Sheet2` tab contained 247 rows with a name in at least one name column. These are different scope claims, not a verified total of unique people or the complete archive. Do not discard historical/inactive names or replace the original roll.

The user subsequently opened the original roll and explicitly authorized inspecting it and duplicating it if edit permission was missing. Browser inspection verified Editor access and Restricted sharing. No duplicate was needed for access, and no cells or permissions were changed. See [source reconciliation](membership-source-reconciliation.md).

`admin/config.js` supports `membershipSheetUrl`, initially blank. Once the church chooses its working ledger and an appropriate private configuration location, staff can be linked to its Google Sheets edit page. Do not commit the private source URL to public app configuration. Google permissions continue to govern access. This link is not synchronization: the history screen and success message explicitly say office edits do not write to Google. Do not publish the membership sheet as CSV to make browser access easier.

Preserve its contents and label until clarified. Before any data migration: inventory exact tabs/headers and row counts; preserve original source numbers and reconcile any generated Ledger MemberIDs through a reviewed crosswalk; reconcile duplicate identities and spelling variants against source pages; count active, inactive and uncertain rows; compare input/output counts; resolve exceptions with the church. A first-time guest is not automatically a member. No real member data has been seeded into app code, screenshots, tests, the vault or this handoff.

## People and historical records

People records now include existing ledger IDs, membership numbers, middle/preferred/former names, household and contact details, historical date text, ways received, removal reasons, and a source-review flag. New records start needing review. Names can retain an unknown surname as blank. Dates such as “summer 1956” or unclear handwriting remain text; a verified exact date is optional in history entries.

Search covers names/variants, member identifiers, household, phone and email. People, documents and history reads page through results with a unique ID as a sorting tie-breaker, rather than silently stopping at the backend's default 1,000 rows. History search covers the loaded entries, dates, details and source labels; the display shows 100 matches at a time with a notice to narrow the search. The member's **History** button filters their entries.

Before saving a new People record, the office offers a review when the loaded People list contains matching nonempty first and last names, or the same nonempty membership number or legacy member ID. These comparisons normalize Unicode composition, capitalization and whitespace while retaining identifier leading zeros. Middle, preferred and former names are not compared. Staff must compare the source before using an existing record or acknowledging that this is a separate person. A changed normalized identity, match set or matching identity details requires a fresh acknowledgment. This is an advisory identity check: a shared name or identifier does not prove two records describe one person, and spelling variants or later changes may be missed. The check does not merge records, enforce database uniqueness, reconcile the historical ledger or establish an import mapping. An absence of matches is not proof that a person is new.

**Open membership spreadsheet** in People & membership or Member history opens the configured working Google Sheet in a separate tab. It preserves the office view and does not copy or change sheet contents. Google account permissions still apply; office edits do not synchronize to that sheet. Keep the real `membershipSheetUrl` in the approved private runtime configuration rather than committing it to the public repository.

The Overview care cards summarize due/overdue plans, unassigned plans and coverage gaps across all loaded, authorized people. These totals ignore the current person, role and search filters. Paused and completed one-time plans are excluded from due/unassigned totals; coverage retains the existing active-person monthly/quarterly rules. Cards open explicit visible queues and preserve unsaved-draft decisions. Unavailable counts render as a dash rather than a false zero. The counts are plans or coverage gaps, not unique people, and do not establish the completeness of a historical import or external notification delivery.

The person's **Care** button opens Guests & care with that person's exact record selected. The selection applies across plans, coverage, guest follow-up and contact history, independently of whether another person shares the same name. **Show everyone** clears the person selection. Care access still follows the existing staff role; this shortcut does not grant additional permissions.

History entries append to a person. Corrections append a new entry linked to the earlier entry and retain its source. The migration blocks ordinary SQL deletion of people and alteration/removal of history/visits. Archive people by changing their current status, keeping historical membership events. This is preservation within the application, not a substitute for an independently verified backup/restore procedure; privileged database operators can override safeguards.

## Handwritten pages

1. Add or identify the person in People. Preserve the original identity and spelling; do not merge on name alone.
2. In Member history choose **Add history / page photo**. On a phone, the image input can use its camera. JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted up to 15 MB; convert HEIC first if needed.
3. Attach the original page or select an existing private source document. Record its book/page reference. Transcribe the wording and mark uncertain letters/dates with `[unclear]`; do not guess missing facts. Several people on one page can reference the same original document.
4. Check the transcription against the image and tick the review checkbox before saving. The original image uses private `church-documents` storage under the reserved `USER-ID/membership-history/` prefix, with generated filenames. That prefix denies viewer access from the start of upload, before a history row exists.
5. A source linked to history is protected against ordinary staff replacement, path changes and deletion. Opening it generates a short-lived private link. Corrections retain earlier entries and their evidence.

Photo upload does not currently run OCR or add rows to Google Sheets. The user may supply photographed pages for assisted transcription once the private destination is accessible; uncertain readings still need review. A future OCR integration should produce a draft/review queue, retain source-page links and require confirmation before ledger writes. Do not send private photos to an unapproved external service or commit them to the repository.

Upload, document metadata and history insertion are separate operations. Session/closed-review guards prevent follow-on work after invalidation. A failed/uncertain request can leave an unlinked private object/document; it is retained rather than risking loss of an original. Review these objects through an approved cleanup process. A retained document can be selected when retrying. Network-uncertain insert outcomes need reconciliation before repeating an entry; no exactly-once import guarantee is claimed.

## Guests and deacon care

**Guests** records the first visit, current next step, status, optional next follow-up date and notes for an existing person. Create their person as `visitor`; linking a guest intake does not change membership status.

**Care plans** now keep separate responsibilities for deacons, Sunday school teachers, welcome, pastoral and additional care. New deacon plans default to three calendar months and teacher plans to one month; a welcome can be a one-time task within two days. Existing day-based plans preserve their previous intervals. Care coverage flags active people missing an assigned recurring plan or exceeding the monthly/quarterly goal. Assignments do not create logins or grant access to records. See [signup and care workflow](signup-care-maintenance.md).

**Visits and contacts** records the care role, who visited/called, the date, method, whether contact succeeded or was only attempted, notes, and an optional explicit next date. Contact history is append-only. Keep notes concise and relevant to the agreed ministry purpose; general People notes remain visible to all approved staff roles, while these care/history tables are restricted to admin/editor.

**Follow-up** uses only contacts for the same person and care role, ignoring future-dated entries for current completion. It uses the most recent relevant visit's explicit next date when present. Otherwise it uses the latest successful contact plus the plan interval, or the plan's start date plus the interval when nobody has been reached. An attempted call does not reset successful-contact age. Paused plans are omitted from the due queue but remain editable. Dates use America/Chicago and remain correct across daylight-saving transitions. Assigned-name and text filters help staff find their list.

This is an on-screen due/overdue queue, recalculated from saved records. It does not send email/text/push reminders or make calls. An approved delivery channel, staff recipients and notification policy are needed before external reminders can be enabled. No recurring Codex automation or message delivery was created.

**Guidelines** has no seeded official policy. The screen labels starter guidance as proposed until an administrator saves the church's actual guidelines. Editors can read them; only admins can revise them. The existing first-time guest sheet and deacon guidelines are still requested from the user.
